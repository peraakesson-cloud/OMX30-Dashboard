# I060 Trickle-vent refresh kit - red-team (demand angle)
Verdict: does not survive as a standalone brand. Revised scores: trend1 def1 margin3 repeat2 eu3 fit3.

Attacks
1. Price anchor: Hornbach lists Schellenberg Universal Fensterlüfter single at 13.95 EUR (search snippet, https://www.hornbach.de/p/universal-fensterluefter-erweiterung-schellenberg-46600-weiss/10529551/ ; not fetched). OBI Plus single 23.99 (https://www.obi.de/p/7415102/schellenberg-fensterluefter-plus-weiss-einzelpack). A 19-25 EUR refresh kit costs as much as a NEW vent. UK frame trickle vents sell for GBP 2.37-11 (https://www.trulypvc.com/window-door-parts/board-sill-trims/simon-framevent-trickle-vent). Replace-not-refresh is the rational buyer choice.
2. No evidence of a replacement-filter category: two searches (UK trickle vent filters; DE Ersatzfilter Schellenberg) found no filter inserts, only a generic eBay Pollenfilter page. Absence = either no demand or fit-fragmentation; both bad. OEM refill (Onninen FI) seen only in search by prior phase.
3. Repeat purchase is invented: an annual refill requires a clogged-filter pain people notice; most trickle vents are unfiltered slots. Repeat 3 is unjustified; realistic 1-2.
4. SAM/SOM entirely ASSUMPTION (5% x 25% x EUR10). Prior analysis itself says no source for vent prevalence. No trend data (Google Trends) was verified by anyone; trend=1 is right but then the whole case is a hunch. Winter condensation is seasonal (Oct-Feb): a 6-month sales window, stock sits idle.
5. CAC: EUR 15-30 vs ~EUR 15-20 ex-VAT revenue = negative contribution; prior analysis admits ~zero before returns. Returns for wrong fit across hundreds of profiles (SE/DK/DE) will be high; compatibility library costs far more than 10 h/week x 2 can build.
6. Landed cost EUR 3-5 and EUR 1,800 first order are unverified; die-cut + brush + packaging + GPSR/EPR compliance per SKU variant multiplies SKUs (each profile = SKU), blowing MOQ budget.
7. Regulatory: GPSR responsible-person/labelling; packaging EPR SE/DK; any airflow/mould claim drifts toward construction-product or biocide/health claims (hard kill). Low-price product cannot absorb compliance overhead.
8. Margin: 75-80% headline margin disappears after 25% VAT, 15% fees, CAC, returns: contribution ~0 (the prior analysis's own number).
Tooling note: Firecrawl credits were exhausted; only two WebSearch queries run, no Trends/Amazon data checked. Confidence Medium-Low.

Copiers: Schellenberg/Volution/VILPE (OEM refills), Chinese die-cut filter sellers on Amazon/AliExpress, any generic pollen-strip seller on eBay/Temu.
Fix: only a pre-sale test (SEO page + Danish/Swedish landing with waitlist, 200 EUR ads) showing <EUR 5 per email and >3% preorder intent; or pivot to B2B bundle with installers/window makers (ex-VELUX network). Otherwise drop.
