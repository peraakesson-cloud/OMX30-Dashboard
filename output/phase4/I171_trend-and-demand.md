My search for a dedicated radiator glove dryer found only generic radiator airers/towel racks (Vileda, Metaltex, WENKO on kaufland.de/ebay.de) - the category is a feature of laundry racks, not a product category.

4. Repeat purchase ~none; durable steel item.
5. Fixed compliance cost + GPSR EU rep + test (EUR 0.5-1k) is large against a EUR 6k budget.
6. Returns/fit: panel radiator profiles vary; review gap (poor fit) is the main return driver and is hard to solve with one SKU.

8. Copy: any Chinese factory copy at EUR 12-18 in weeks; electric ADAX shelves occupy premium (unverified price).
