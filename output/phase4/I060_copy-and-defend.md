# I060 Trickle-vent refresh kit - red-team (copy and defend)

Research limits: web search tool (Firecrawl) was out of credits during this pass, so no new URLs were fetched. Claims below are reasoning from prior-phase URLs plus labelled ASSUMPTIONS. Confidence: Low-Medium.

## Strongest attacks
1. Fatal margin/CAC: EUR 19-25 retail incl. 25% VAT = ~EUR 15-20 ex-VAT. After 15% fees, 3PL pick/pack (~EUR 2-3, ASSUMPTION), postage in a letterbox envelope to SE/DK (~EUR 3-6, ASSUMPTION), returns for wrong fit, and CAC EUR 15-30, contribution <= 0 on first order. The "65-80% gross margin" counts only product cost and ignores fulfilment, which for a <EUR 20 item is the same size as landed cost.
2. Replacement vent undercuts: whole Schellenberg vent at EUR 23.99 (OBI link in prior analysis) = price of the kit. The buyer's alternative is "buy a new vent", not "buy a refill".
3. Copy in <6 months: a die-cutter and a fit table. Likely copiers: generic Amazon.de/.se sellers of foam/pollen filter strips, AliExpress/Temu cut-to-size filter foam (ASSUMPTION, not verified), Volution/FRESH and VILPE selling OEM refills (Onninen FI listing seen in search only), Schellenberg. Clas Ohlson / Jula / Biltema could add a private-label "ventilfilter" at EUR 5-10 trivially (ASSUMPTION; not verified). IKEA/Normann/Hay/Flying Tiger are unlikely - not a design object.
4. Moat is weak: compatibility library is public information (profile dimensions), and a dull, low-involvement product has no brand pull. Refill subscription for a EUR 12 filter has negative LTV once ad/shipping costs are included.
5. Demand unproven: every TAM input is an ASSUMPTION; EUR 10-30k year-1 revenue does not repay 10h/week of founder time.
6. Capital claim: EUR 1,800 for 500 kits is plausible for die-cut parts, but that is ~EUR 3.6/kit; verified quote absent. Capital is not the constraint; margin and CAC are.
7. Brand/regulatory friction: ventilation/airflow claims, GPSR responsible person, packaging EPR in SE/DK add fixed cost on tiny volume.

## Revised scores (1-5)
trend 1, defensibility 1, margin 2, repeat 2, eu 4, fit 3.

## Verdict
Does not survive. Fix only by repositioning as B2B (window installers/housing associations, 1000+ units, EUR 3-6 per unit multi-pack) or bundling inside a larger ventilation/anti-condensation product.
