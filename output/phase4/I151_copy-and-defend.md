## Strongest attacks
1. Price per unit is ~2x the closest incumbent. Jackson Galaxy sells TWO refillable silvervine toys for USD 18.99 (https://jacksongalaxy.com/products/twin-critters-refillable-silvervine-sardines-set-of-2); Space Kitty Express 2-pack mice also USD 18.99 (https://www.jacksongalaxy.com/products/space-kitty-express-catnip-alternative-2-pack-mice); Spot single is USD 12.99 at Target (https://www.target.com/p/spot-silver-vine-refillable-cat-toy-assorted-characters/-/A-87419393). A single EUR 19 toy needs a design premium of ~2x; unproven.
2. Existing product already is the concept (refillable + silvervine + giftable set; SheKnows editorial coverage https://www.sheknows.com/living/articles/2695668/twincritters-silver-vine-cat-toys/). Jackson Galaxy has brand + distribution in US; EU entry is a trivial extension.
3. Gross margin claim is on the flattering base. 74-84% is net-of-VAT product margin vs landed cost. Fully loaded: net 15.2, landed 2.5-4 (ASSUMPTION, no supplier quote), shipping 4-5, fees 3%, returns/breakage, so contribution ~7-8 BEFORE ads = ~50% of net revenue. With CAC EUR 20-35 first order loses ~EUR 12-27.
4. Amazon EU: referral for general pet supplies stays ~15% (France pet shop 15% + EUR 0.30 per item per search result, sell.amazon.fr; Germany rate not verified); 2026 cut applies to pet food/clothing only (https://globalpetindustry.com/news/analysis-amazon-reduces-marketplace-fees-for-low-value-goods-in-europe/). On EUR 19 gross: ~2.85 referral + FBA small-parcel ~3-4 (ASSUMPTION) + landed 3 + ads 15% (2.85) leaves ~EUR 6-7 before VAT (DE 19%: ~3.0 VAT, so net 16.0). Thin and no repeat data ownership.
5. Retail later breaks the 3x rule. Wholesale at ~50% of ex-VAT retail = ~7.6 vs landed ~3 = 2.5x, margin ~60%: below both targets. Retail to Clas Ohlson/Jula/Flying Tiger would only be viable at <= EUR 2.5 landed; unverified.
6. Refill is the weak half: EUR 10 incl VAT (8 net), letterbox post 2.5-3, below the EUR 15 retail kill floor stand-alone; loose silvervine/matatabi sticks cost EUR 2.49-7.29 at zooplus (https://www.zooplus.de/shop/katzen/katzenspielzeug/baldrian_katzenspielzeug/silver_vine/2006676) and refills USD 3.99 per 3 (Whisker City, PetSmart). Refill price anchor is ~EUR 1.3 each; EUR 10 refill is ~7x.
7. Subscription fantasy: silvervine potency lasts weeks; most cats ignore or destroy plush in days (Chewy reviews cited in prior phase). Churn unsourced; plausible <3 refills.
8. SOM and CAC are all assumptions; 10 h/week cannot feed TikTok content plus CS plus 3PL.

## What really protects it
Nothing structural: no patent (pocket + pouch), no tooling, no regulatory barrier, no network effect. Only brand/design taste and community; founder design edge is real but unproven in pet.
