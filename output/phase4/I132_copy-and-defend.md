## Attacks
1. Copy risk (serious/fatal for defensibility): a Nordic layout is a PDF. Likely copiers within 6 months: Burde (already has Swedish dagbok), Etsy/print-on-demand sellers (printable Swedish/Danish planner inserts), Wonderland 222 and other Tomoe-style US/EU brands adding SE/DK calendar inserts, Paper Whisper/Hobonichi distribution adding Nordic inserts, Papier (UK, EU shipping). Chains like Flying Tiger/Clas Ohlson/Jula/IKEA would not copy a premium thin-paper niche but own the seasonal EUR 10-20 diary shelf. AliExpress/Temu sell A6 thin-paper planner clones at EUR 5-10 (ASSUMPTION, not verified). Nothing protects: no patent, design right weak on layouts, paper is commodity, community lock-in needs scale a 10h/week team lacks.
2. Unit economics are thinner than shown: margin is computed before fulfilment. Landed EUR 9 is an unverified screen guess (thin paper, ~368 pp, hardbound/cover, custom print at 500 units; MOQ for Tomoe-grade paper unknown). 28/9 = 3.1x leaves zero slack on the 3x rule; at landed EUR 11 it fails (2.5x). Subtract 3PL pick/pack + SE/DK postage (~EUR 5-8, ASSUMPTION), payment fees ~3%, returns/damaged in gift season (~3-8%, ASSUMPTION) and contribution before ads is ~EUR 9-12; paid CAC EUR 15-25 means every first order loses money. Wholesale at 50% of retail gives ~36% margin, failing the 65% rule; Amazon adds ~15% referral + FBA, also failing.
3. Capital: EUR 500 units x EUR 9 = 4.5k is plausible, but a day-per-page edition needs 2 SKUs (cover + refill), 2 languages, samples, EPR/GPSR, and unsold stock expires with the year (dated product: unsold 2028 stock is worth ~0 after Jan). Seasonal Aug-Jan means one shot per year; 2027 window gone (today 2026-11-11), so first revenue is ~Aug 2027 at best, cash tied ~9 months.
4. Demand: Hobonichi 1M copies is Japan-driven; Nordic SAM is ASSUMPTION only. Y1 EUR 10-25k revenue means gross profit ~EUR 7-17k before ads: roughly zero founder return. Nordic buyers also use phones/Google Calendar; local week-number/holiday need is already served by Burde at EUR 12-35.
5. Defensibility claim in prior score (2) is fair but total 3.14 is generous given fit and trend.

Does not survive as a standalone product. Only if fixed: lock in exclusive Nordic distribution/licence of an existing Japanese or US brand (no own design), or add a defensible hardware element (proprietary cover system, designer licences with exclusivity), get a binding paper+print quote showing landed <= EUR 7, and pass a pre-sale (not waitlist) test of 150+ paid preorders.
