# RED-TEAM N034 - Moat and copy attack (2026-11-11)
Evidence status: WebSearch budget exhausted this turn; no URLs fetched. All points below are reasoning/memory, UNVERIFIED (Low confidence) unless stated.

## Attacks
1. FATAL-ish: A licence is a contract, not a moat. It binds only the licensor. Anyone can copy the object if copyright is lapsed (life+70: designers d.1957-1995 are mostly still in copyright to 2027-2065, so rights exist, but only for original works) or if the object is not original enough. Applied-art protection after Cofemel (C-683/17) / Mio-konektra (C-580/23, C-795/23) is real but requires originality and is litigated case by case (memory, unverified). Small utilitarian objects (trays, candle holders, bowls) are the weakest.
2. Exclusive licence vs. incumbents: the desirable estates (Jacobsen, Bojesen, Aalto, Frank, Wegner) are already tied up by Rosendahl, Stelton, Artek, Svenskt Tenn, Carl Hansen (memory). Left: second-tier names with low recognition, so the licence protects a product nobody searches for.
3. Style-alike routing: IKEA, Hay, Normann, Bloomingville, Broste, Flying Tiger, Clas Ohlson, and AliExpress/Temu sellers copy aesthetics freely; the licence blocks only the exact object. Marketplace copies appear in weeks; Amazon EU enforcement is a notice-and-takedown, costing founder hours.
4. Moat is term-limited (3-5 yr) and renewal is at the estate's discretion; once the product proves demand, estate can sell to an incumbent. Founders have no leverage after year 1.
5. Capital: advance 1.5-3k + tooling 1.5-3k + first run + legal is 9-11k, i.e. at/over the 10k kill line, with year-1 demand of 200-400 units unvalidated and unsourced.
6. Unit economics: EUR 79 incl. 25% VAT, net 63.2; royalty, 3PL, returns leave ~25 contribution; paid CAC 25-40 yields ~0 or negative first order. Wholesale ~7/unit. Repeat is near zero (collectible one-off, no consumable). Freight/3PL figures all ASSUMPTION; Amazon fees (~15% referral + FBA) not applicable to a story-led SKU.
7. Founder fit: building-industry and cleaning-robotics edges are unused; B2B channel relationship is design shops/estates, which founders do not have.
8. Provenance moat is weak: "numbered edition + certificate" is trivially replicable by a style-alike brand and not protected.

## Likely copiers
Hay, Normann Copenhagen, Bloomingville/Broste, IKEA (inspired lines), Clas Ohlson/Jula for low-end, AliExpress/Temu/Amazon sellers for visual clones, incumbent licensees (Rosendahl, Stelton, Audo, Carl Hansen) who can relicense the estate later. Time to route around: weeks (clones) to a year (relicence).

## Verdict
Survives only if fixed: sign a named object with registered EU design/trade mark held in estate and licensee names, with renewal option, before spending money.
