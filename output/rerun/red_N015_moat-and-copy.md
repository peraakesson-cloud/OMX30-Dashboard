# Red team N015 BRF Ventilation Year-Pack - moat and copy
Date 2026-11-11. Fresh WebSearch not possible (shared 200-search budget exhausted); only URLs already in the prior analysis were reused, none re-verified. EUIPO/Espacenet NOT searched.

## Verdict: does not survive as a product business. Only as a near-zero-capital B2B demand test.
1. No mechanism. Moat = relationships + size schedule. Not registrable (a size list is data; a standard filter pad is not a novel design), no proprietary material, no consumable lock-in (compatible with Fresh/Flexit by design). Ceiling defensibility 1-2.
2. Test report is a negative moat: any competitor can commission EN ISO 16890 for a few k EUR; cost unquoted (ASSUMPTION).
3. Real copier = the incumbent in the building: ventilation service/OVK firms (e.g. Kiwa as inspector, URL in prior brief), property managers and caretakers add filters to existing contracts at near-zero marginal cost. Time to copy: one contract cycle (months), no tooling.
4. Retail anchor: Fresh 3-pack SEK 80-109 (~EUR 7-10) (Bygghemma / Hornbach URLs in prior brief, snippet-only). 3x premium with no product difference; boards compare. Jula/Biltema/Clas Ohlson/Amazon sellers/Temu can list generic pads; only share-of-wallet is small, but they cap the price.
5. Buyer/payer mismatch: in-apartment filter changes are often the resident's duty per bylaws (familjensjurist.se URL in prior brief), so the BRF may not pay at all.
6. Economics: EUR 1,000 per 40-apt BRF, ~EUR 640 pre-CAC; founder time 10-15 h per BRF at 10 h/week total = ~1 BRF/week at best, annual-meeting seasonality; first revenue spring-summer 2027. 100 BRFs ~EUR 64k/yr is a multi-year build. Landed cost EUR 7.5/apt looks high vs retail EUR 2-3.5 per filter piece (SEK 36/piece Hornbach), so margin may be better but price is anyway capped. Amazon fees/FBA irrelevant (B2B), 3PL/returns minor.
7. Hard-rule fit: it is a retail-priced low-ticket commodity (EUR 7 per unit) bundled; per-unit price under EUR 15 is near a hard kill unless sold strictly as a service subscription.

## Likely copiers
Local ventilation service firms/OVK inspectors; property managers (forvaltare); Fresh/Flexit/Swegon/Polarpumpen OEMs; Amazon/Temu generic filter sellers; Biltema/Jula/Clas Ohlson/Bygghemma private label.

## Revised scores (1-5)
trend 2, defensibility 1, margin 3, repeat 4, eu 2, fit 3, channel_econ 2 -> ~2.4
## Fix
Make it a paid service (survey + schedule + documentation) with a property-manager framework agreement, or fold into an exclusive filter format only with a registered design/OEM licence. Otherwise kill. Validate with LOIs before any spend.
