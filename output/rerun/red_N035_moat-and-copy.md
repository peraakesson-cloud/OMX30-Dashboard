# Red-team N035 Exclusive-artist wooden jigsaw: moat and copy angle

Evidence status: WebSearch budget exhausted (200/turn); no URLs seen. Everything below is reasoning or ASSUMPTION, Low confidence. EUIPO/Espacenet not checked.

## Attacks
1. FATAL-ish: the licence moat protects the artist's images, not the product. Wooden laser-cut puzzles are a commodity technique (many Etsy/AliExpress sellers); any competitor signs a different Nordic illustrator in weeks. The moat blocks one artist only, and artist-for-hire is non-exclusive across categories.
2. Artist holds leverage: royalty/advance renegotiation at renewal, 3-year term, artist can jump to a larger publisher. Founders build the audience, artist takes the asset back.
3. No registrable mechanism: laser-cut puzzle has no patentable/utility-model feature; piece-cut pattern could be a registered design but is trivially designed around. Copyright over artwork is automatic (no registration), but enforcement vs Temu/AliExpress clones costs more than the whole budget.
4. Copy speed: marketplace sellers can lift the artwork (print-on-demand puzzles) within weeks of any viral post; EU takedowns on Amazon/Temu are slow and founder hours (10/week) cannot police them.
5. Economics: 79 EUR is a big ask for an unknown brand; 38% contribution before CAC; paid CAC 25-45 makes first order loss-making; dependence on artist audience conversion (unproven). Wholesale at 50% margin leaves about 0 after returns. Q4 seasonality means no sales before late 2027 realistically; cash tied EUR 8-10k.
6. Landed-cost: wood cut in EU vs Asia; Asia sample QC risk, burnt smell, formaldehyde/REACH tests (EUR 300-800 ASSUMPTION), GPSR responsible-person duty.

## Likely copiers
Wentworth, Liberty and Nervous System (premium wooden; sign same or similar artists); Etsy/Temu/AliExpress laser-cut sellers; Ravensburger/Clementoni wooden lines; Nordic gift retailers (Hay, Normann, Flying Tiger, Clas Ohlson, Jula) could license a puzzle with their own illustrator in one season; museum shops. IKEA/Biltema unlikely at 79 EUR. All unverified.

## Verdict
Survives only if fixed: moat is a contract with a single artist, not a mechanism.

## Fix
Make exclusivity category-wide and long (5 yr + option), co-ownership or equity-style royalty for artist, registered Community design on the piece-cut pattern (about EUR 350 filing, ASSUMPTION), bundle a proprietary-format element (e.g., numbered frame/display tray) and a B2B channel (hotels, architects, building-industry gifting from VELUX network) with pre-sales proving 60+ orders at CAC under 20 before any spend.
