# Red team N020 KeyPouch - moat and copy (2026-11-11)
Research status: WebSearch budget exhausted, packagingeurope.com not resolvable, Firecrawl unavailable. NO new sources verified this run. All points below are reasoning/ASSUMPTION, confidence Low-Medium.

## Attacks
1. Fatal-ish: keyed interface is a technically-dictated/must-fit feature (EU Design Reg Art 8(1)/(2) in spirit); registered design protects appearance only, so a different key shape/colour avoids it. Design-around 1-3 months with a 3D printer, not 6-12. Sweden has no utility model; DK UM covers only mechanical solutions, needs novelty vs prior art (many lockable/keyed cartridge dispensers exist: Tork, Katrin, Meadow KAPSUL - unverified here). Patent route EUR 5-10k+ - over budget.
2. Lock-in is a buyer objection, not a moat: small hotels buy via wholesalers (Lyreco, Staples, Hotel supply) at EUR 3-5/L (ASSUMPTION); keyed refill at EUR 8-12/L is a visible 2-3x premium on a commodity, resisted by owners and by distributors who lose margin.
3. Named incumbents already own the pattern: Meadow KAPSUL (SE, same market), Tork/Essity, Katrin, Kimberly-Clark Prof., Deb/SC Johnson, Bobrick, Madara HoReCa. They have distributor reach and can undercut. Amazon/AliExpress sellers copy hardware in weeks; locking dispensers are ~USD 28-35 retail (WebstaurantStore Paya listing, seen in earlier screen). IKEA/Jula/Biltema/Clas Ohlson/Hay/Normann are unlikely copiers for B2B but could sell a cheap lockable dispenser to consumers.
4. Third-party compatible refills: key geometry on the pouch is trivially replicated by any filler; a copied key pouch at EUR 5/L undercuts. Enforcement across SE/DK/EU for EUR 5-10k startup is unrealistic (litigation EUR 50k+).
5. Economics: capital EUR 11-14k (prior analysis) breaches soft cap; cosmetics RP/CPNP/GMP filler MOQs, shelf-life, leaking pouches, freight of liquids (heavy: 1 L ~1.1 kg, 3PL pick fees), returns of leaking product, B2B payment terms. Year-1 SOM EUR 25-45k revenue; gross profit after CAC EUR 4-17k vs 500+ founder hours; consumable volume per account (~130 L/yr) is small so repeat revenue is ~EUR 300-500/account.
6. Regulatory tailwind uncertain: PPWR minis restriction timing (2030) unverified; hotels may go bulk with any refillable bottle (no keyed system needed). Tailwind benefits all competitors.
7. Moat is really installed base + service, which a bootstrapped team cannot build fast; distributors gate access, founders' ex-Nilfisk janitorial contacts help only partly.

Survives only if fixed. Fix: sign distributor/chain exclusivity or white-label deal first (B2B moat), get IP attorney opinion before tooling, use off-the-shelf dispenser + custom keyed cap (<EUR 1k), pitch cost-per-room-night savings not lock-in, price refill <= EUR 6/L.
