## Regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- **No CE or medical-device regime for the filter itself** (domain knowledge, not re-verified). Do not make health or allergy-relief claims.
- **Class claims:** only state "F7/ePM1 xx%" if backed by an ISO 16890 report on the actual media/construction. Misstated class is a consumer-protection and B2B liability risk. Which sizes a report covers (one report per media vs per size) is UNVERIFIED, so ask the lab.
- **RISE P-mark (optional):** RISE offers [P-marking of air filters](https://ri.se/en/what-we-do/services/p-marking-of-air-filters) and [filter testing](https://www.ri.se/en/what-we-do/services/testing-of-air-filters); cost and time not seen. It requires annual factory inspection of the manufacturer, so it adds a recurring cost and supplier dependency.
- **GPSR:** importer/manufacturer identity and traceability on pack.
- **Packaging EPR/producer responsibility** in Sweden and Denmark (registration with the national schemes; not researched, ASSUMPTION it applies to D2C).
- **Trademark:** use OEM names only as "compatible with" (nominative use); never imply OEM approval.
- **Warranty:** OEMs (Genvex) say only originals keep performance; EU rules generally prevent tying warranty to branded spares, but this is not verified here. Position as "certified alternative" and provide a fit guarantee.
- **Subscriptions (D2C):** EU consumer-law rules for recurring orders and cancellation (domain knowledge, not re-verified).
- Not applicable: batteries, electrical CE, REACH SVHC (unless adhesives or coatings are added; check media datasheet).

## The moat, specifically
Three layers, honestly rated:

1. **ISO 16890 test report / optional RISE P-mark.**
   - *How:* send a media sample/filter to an accredited lab (RISE or another); get a class report (ePM1/ePM10 efficiency). Cost and time NOT quoted (ASSUMPTION EUR 2-5k per media/construction family, 4-10 weeks; P-mark adds annual inspection fees). Get at least two written quotes before committing.
   - *Circumvention:* any competitor can commission the same test on their own media for similar cost; Camfil/Eurovent-certified incumbents already publish reports. It is a **barrier to entry for installers who want documentation, not exclusivity**. Time for a copyist: ~2-4 months (ASSUMPTION).
2. **Installer price list plus subscription inertia.**
   - *How:* signed installer programme, a pack-labelling and reorder portal, subscription base.
   - *Circumvention:* another distributor with a bigger installer catalogue or an OEM loyalty programme. This is a relationship moat that depends on founders actually executing; it is real only after 10+ active installers.
3. **EU registered design on the frame.**
   - *How:* EUIPO filing; fees changed on 1 July 2026 ([Mondaq](https://www.mondaq.com/trademark/1819032/eu-design-law-the-5-major-changes-to-know-about-eu-designs-and-models-from-1-july-2026), not re-read); earlier assumed ~EUR 350 per design (Low confidence).
   - *Weakness:* a frame that must fit an OEM housing is largely dictated by function and fit; EU design law excludes features solely dictated by technical function and, under the 2024 reform, includes a repair/spare-part clause (domain knowledge, unverified here). Expect narrow, easily designed-around protection. Count it as marginal.

**Net:** defensibility 2. A competitor with an installer base could match it in under 6 months; a new entrant without a base needs 6-12 months to build the base.

## Biggest risk
Installers and owners do not switch from the OEM set at a lower price, because (a) OEM trade terms and warranty fear dominate and (b) compatible filters already exist, so the premium for "certified" is smaller than assumed. Second: the ISO 16890 report costs more or covers fewer sizes than assumed.

## Cheapest validation test (about EUR 300-450, 4-6 weeks, no stock, no lab fee)
1. Get **written quotes** from RISE and one other lab for the ISO 16890 report and P-mark (free). Kill if the report for 3 SKUs plus P-mark first-year exceeds EUR 5k.
2. **15-20 installer/service-firm calls** through the founders' network. Ask OEM trade price, margin, who buys the filters, would they carry a certified alternative. Pass: at least 5 say yes in writing for a trial of 20 sets each at EUR 24 trade (i.e. ~100 sets).
3. **Landing page + EUR 200 Google Shopping/search test** for 3 Flexit/Genvex model families, with pre-order/waitlist at EUR 39. Pass: at least 25 waitlist sign-ups or 10 pre-orders (cost per sign-up under EUR 10). Kill if CPC and sign-up cost imply CAC above EUR 25.
4. Buy two retail OEM sets (EUR 60-100) to measure frame/fit and see what the packaging claims.

Decision rule: continue only if (1) lab quote under EUR 5k, (2) five installers commit, and (3) D2C sign-up cost is under EUR 10.
