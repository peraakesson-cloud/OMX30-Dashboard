# I075 Nordic Light-Seal Kit (roof-window blackout edge seal) - deep analysis (re-run, 2026-11-11)

**Verdict: PARK / conditional test only.** Real, observable pain and a good founder channel fit, but the moat is thin (defensibility 2, not 3), the TAM is small and unsourced, and paid-social economics are negative. Only worth a sub-EUR-600 B2B/pre-order test.

Evidence rules: URLs below were seen in search results this session (not all were fetched; search snippets only). Anything else is labelled ASSUMPTION. amazon.de and Firecrawl were not used. No VELUX sales volumes or installed-base counts could be found.

## 1. Product and what the "pain" really is
A kit of edge seals/frames that closes the 1-2 cm light gap between a roof-window sash and a roller blackout blind's side channels. VELUX itself says the fabric is 100% blackout but the blind must sit tight against the sash and channels, and light leaks are a fitting issue ([VELUX support](https://www.velux.co.uk/support/wiki/light-coming-through-blackout-blind)). Customer reviews report leaks on all edges even when fittings are tight ([review](https://reviews.customergauge.com/veluxblindsdirectcouk/TTXyqTpxffgWUf98)); a DIY blogger fixed triangular top leaks with draught-excluder strips ([blog](https://nik-sargent.squarespace.com/blog/tag/diy)). So the pain exists, but the existing evidence is anecdotal (3 reviews), and a DIY 5-euro strip is the real substitute.

## 6. Channels and CAC (ASSUMPTIONS unless noted)
- **Paid social/Shopify (DK/SE):** CAC EUR 20-35 for a EUR 31 ex-VAT product; conversion hurt by small niche targeting and the need to confirm window code.
- **Installers/roofers (founder network):** CAC ~0 cash, founder time; 1 installer might use 20-50 kits/yr.
- **Merchants (Ahlsell-type, local roofing merchants):** wholesale ~50% of retail ex VAT; listing takes months; need EAN, packaging, pallet min.
- **Housing associations:** project sales; long cycle.
- **SEO "Velux lys siden/ljus runt rullgardin"** long-tail; cheapest scalable organic route, low volume.

## 7. Regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- GPSR (Regulation 2023/988): responsible EU economic operator, traceability, safety info on product.
- REACH/ CLP for adhesives and textile/foam; no SVHC; adhesive must not damage frames (consumer-claims risk).
- Flammability: no mandatory EU rule for this item, but check bedroom/housing association tender expectations (ASSUMPTION).
- Blind-cord safety EN 13120 relevant if a cord/pull is supplied; a passive seal should not add loops/cords.
- Trademark: use "compatible with VELUX" only referentially. EUTMR Art. 14(1)(c) allows accessory use if necessary and honest ([summary](https://cms.law/en/gbr/legal-updates/pre-branded-spare-parts-cjeu-has-its-say-in-audi-trade-mark-case)); VELUX dealer guidelines restrict how dealers use the mark ([VELUX](https://resources.velux.co.uk/digital-guidelines/follow-the-guidelines)). Do not imply endorsement; do not use VELUX in brand/domain. No DK/SE case law found.
- Electrical/batteries/medical: N/A (do not claim sleep/health benefits).
- VAT: DK and SE 25% (ASSUMPTION from general knowledge); OSS for cross-border distance sales.

## 8. The moat, sceptically
Stated moat: B2B channel + per-window-model fit database; no patent.
- **Channel relationships:** real but personal; a competitor like a blinds maker can ring the same merchants. Also an ex-employer conflict risk (non-competes, goodwill with VELUX reps) that I cannot assess.
- **Fit database:** takes weeks of measurement with real windows, then copyable by reverse-measuring. Time-to-copy: months.
- **EU registered design:** EUR 350 filing ([HGF](https://www.hgf.com/knowledge-hub/legal-updates/re-defining-design-protection-in-the-european-union-what-it-means-for-your-business/)); but a design for a purely technical-function strip is weakly protectable (ASSUMPTION), easy to design around. Danish utility model possible; Sweden has no utility model (my knowledge, not verified); cost not found. A patent is likely above the EUR 5k kill line.
- **Independent test:** a lux/light-tightness test report (e.g. at a Nordic test institute, ASSUMPTION EUR 1-3k) is a marketing asset, not a lock; competitors can pay too.
- **VELUX can neutralise it** by redesigning channels or selling its own seal, and its blind is the host product.
Conclusion: defensibility **2**. Circumvention: third-party blind makers copy in 3-6 months; DIY strips substitute today.

## 9. Unit economics (EUR, per kit, ASSUMPTIONS except where sourced)
- Retail EUR 35 incl. 25% VAT => EUR 28 ex VAT (D2C). Landed cost EUR 7 (record: 6-9; unverified). Payment/platform ~EUR 1.2. Pick/pack + parcel DK/SE ~EUR 5. **D2C contribution ~EUR 14.8**.
- Paid CAC EUR 20-35 => **contribution after CAC = -EUR 5 to -20 per first order**.
- B2B: wholesale EUR 14 ex VAT (merchant sells at 35 incl. VAT); landed 7; freight/pack ~EUR 1 per unit in bulk => **~EUR 6 per kit**. Installer direct at EUR 20: ~EUR 12. CAC ~0 cash but ~3-5 founder-hours per account; at 30 kits/account/yr = EUR 180-360/yr per account => EUR 40-80/hour of founder time at best. Need ~150 B2B accounts to reach EUR 30k contribution: unrealistic at 10 h/week.
- Repeat: one-off (kit per window; add-on of 1-3 windows). Low.
- First order: ~1,000 kits x EUR 7 = EUR 7k + tooling (die-cut/extrusion ~EUR 1-3k, ASSUMPTION) => inside soft limit but ~30% of capital for a EUR 5-15k-contribution pool.

## 11. Biggest risk
Demand is too small/shallow: customers compare a EUR 35 fix against a EUR 9 strip or a EUR 29-80 new blind, and VELUX can respond.

## 12. Cheapest validation test (under EUR 600, 3-4 weeks)
1. Make 20 hand-built prototypes (felt/brush seal + adhesive tape strips) for 3-4 popular window codes (~EUR 250).
2. Measure lux with and without (phone/lux meter EUR 20) in 5 founder-network homes; photograph before/after.
3. Present to 10 installers/merchant contacts; target: 3 agree to a 50-unit trial at EUR 14-20 wholesale.
4. Landing page + EUR 150 ads in DK/SE homeowner/parent groups with pre-order at EUR 29 (refundable). Pass: 30 pre-orders, or 3 installer commitments. Fail otherwise: stop.
