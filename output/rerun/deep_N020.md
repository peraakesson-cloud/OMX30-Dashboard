## 3. Personas
1. **Owner-operator of a 8-30 room independent hotel/B&B (SE/DK)**: buys minis or hotel-grade bottles; wants cheap, tidy, not-stolen, "sustainable" for booking-site badges. Buys after a visible sample.
2. **Hostel manager (30-150 beds)**: price per night and theft dominate; likes locking. Low willingness to pay for design.
3. **STR / serviced-apartment property manager (10-100 units)**: wants fewer SKUs, a restock subscription, consistent look; the most repeatable buyer.
4. **Influencer on the sale: housekeeping lead / janitorial distributor**: decides refill logistics; may block anything not in their catalogue.

## 4. Jobs to be done
- Stay compliant and "green" without losing the guest experience.
- Stop shampoo theft and half-empty minis (cost per room-night).
- Restock fast: one SKU, one delivery, no decanting by housekeeping.
- Look premium enough to protect review scores.

## 8. Regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- **Cosmetics Regulation (EC) 1223/2009** if pouches are filled with shampoo/gel/soap: Responsible Person, safety report, CPNP notification, labelling in local language, GMP (ISO 22716) filler. Avoid "antibacterial" claims (would pull in the Biocidal Products Regulation). Safest start: dispenser plus empty or partner-filled pouch, filler or partner as Responsible Person.
- **CLP (EC) 1272/2008** only if a formulation is classified hazardous (usually not).
- **PPWR (EU) 2025/40**: pouch is packaging; producer registration/EPR in Sweden and Denmark (commercial packaging rules differ; details unverified); recyclability/design-for-recycling expectations from ~2030 favour mono-material PE pouches over multilayer laminate.
- **GPSR (EU) 2023/988** for the dispenser (guest-accessible product): safety info, traceability, EU contact.
- **REACH** substance-of-concern information for plastics; RoHS/WEEE/CE not applicable (no electronics).
- Not applicable: medical device rules, batteries, food contact.

## 10. Unit economics (ASSUMPTION throughout, 20-room account)
Hardware: sell EUR 29-39 (use 35); landed ~EUR 7-10 plus outbound freight and fees ~EUR 2-3, so contribution ~EUR 24 each, ~EUR 480 for 20.
Pouches: price EUR 8 (base) at ~130 L/yr; cost filled EUR 3.5-5 + pack/ship ~EUR 1; contribution ~EUR 2-3/L base, ~EUR 260-390/yr per account. At EUR 10-12 it is ~EUR 4-6/L (EUR 520-780). If the market forces EUR 6/L it is ~EUR 0.5-1.5/L, near break-even.

## 12. Verdict
Conditional: viable niche, not a proven moat. Do not commit tooling or the first order yet. Biggest risk: **buyers reject paying a lock-in premium (EUR 8-12/L vs generic EUR 3-5/L) and the keyed design right cannot be enforced**, leaving a commodity dispenser. Second: total capital ~EUR 11-14k exceeds the stated budget.
