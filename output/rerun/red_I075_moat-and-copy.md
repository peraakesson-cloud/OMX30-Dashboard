# Red-team I075 Nordic Light-Seal Kit - moat and copy angle (2026-11-11)

Research limits: WebSearch budget exhausted; velux.dk and hgf.com not resolvable from this session. NO fresh sources were fetched. Everything below is reasoning, all Low confidence, labelled ASSUMPTION unless it repeats prior-phase URLs. EUIPO/Espacenet prior-art search NOT performed.

## Attacks
1. Moat is not a moat (fatal). Prior analysis itself concedes no patent, weak registered design, test report is not a lock, fit data is re-measurable. Required rerun test (concrete mechanism) is failed by the candidate's own admission. Defensibility 1-2.
2. A registered design on a strip protects appearance only; function-dictated features are excluded (EU Design Reg. Art. 8(1)). A copier changes profile/colour. Design-around cost ~EUR 0 (ASSUMPTION).
3. Copy speed: generic EPDM/foam/brush/velcro strips and blackout side-channel tape are standard commodity items. Likely copiers: Amazon EU 3P sellers and AliExpress/Temu (weeks), blind makers selling "light-tight" kits (Blinds-2go, Aurora, Hornbach Soluna per prior competitor list), Biltema/Jula/Clas Ohlson (could source a generic window-seal strip; ASSUMPTION, not checked), and VELUX itself (can add side channels or a seal accessory; DKL line already exists). IKEA/Hay/Normann/Flying Tiger unlikely - wrong category.
4. Unit economics: EUR 35 price vs EUR 3-10 DIY strip substitute; paid CAC EUR 20-35 per prior analysis gives negative first-order contribution. Amazon route: ~15% referral + FBA fees + returns for fit errors (window-code fit is a return driver). Landed EUR 7 is unverified; a 20-30 piece MOQ pack of die-cut/printed strips with per-window-code SKUs multiplies SKU count and inventory risk. Small kit parcels to DK/SE: ~EUR 4-6 shipping (ASSUMPTION).
5. B2B channel is the only real asset but it is a relationship, not a moat: merchants/installers can buy the same strip from a Chinese supplier at EUR 2-3 after seeing the sample (ASSUMPTION) and the founders' VELUX ties create conflict-of-interest/trademark exposure with the very brand whose windows the kit patches.
6. Tiny and unquantified market: no installed-base data; prior SOM EUR 10-30k gross. First order ~EUR 7k + tooling for a EUR 10-30k year is poor ROI.
7. Defensibility via consumable lock-in is absent: one-off fix, no repeat.

## Likely copiers
Amazon EU 3P/private-label sellers; AliExpress/Temu strip vendors; roof-window blind makers (Blinds-2go, Aurora, Soluna/Hornbach); Nordic DIY chains (Biltema, Jula, Clas Ohlson - assumption); VELUX/FAKRO (FAKRO sells own blinds - assumption, unverified). Route-around time: 1-3 months.

## Revised scores (1-5)
trend 2, defensibility 1, margin 3, repeat 1, eu 2, fit 3, channel_econ 2. Survives: no.

## What would fix it
A real mechanism: a utility-model/patent-able clamp-in profile with a distinct geometry (cost >EUR 5k = kill) or an exclusive supply/distribution deal with a roof-window or blind maker (VELUX/Fakro licence, selling as their accessory). Without that, run only the EUR 600 test as a B2B/installer probe, or drop.
