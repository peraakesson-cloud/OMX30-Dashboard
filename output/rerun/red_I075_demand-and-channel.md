Research limits: WebSearch budget exhausted, Firecrawl out of credits. NO fresh sources fetched. All attacks are from reasoning on the prior analysis's own numbers; competitor URLs in the prior file were NOT re-verified by me.

## Attacks
1. FATAL: B2B channel does not carry the volume. Prior file's own maths: ~150 installer/merchant accounts for EUR 30k contribution, at 3-5 founder-hours per account = 450-750 h, vs 10 h/week (~520 h/year combined, nothing left for ops). Installers sell one-off fixes; no repeat.
2. SERIOUS: D2C paid CAC (assumed EUR 20-35) exceeds contribution (~EUR 14.8 at EUR 35 retail); prior file says -5 to -20 per first order. Only repeat or B2B rescues it and repeat is ~0 (one-off per window, no consumable).
3. SERIOUS: Moat is none. Prior file admits no patent, weak registered design, 3-6 month copy time. Per the rerun rules this is defensibility 1-2.
4. SERIOUS: Demand is a niche symptom of an existing product. Substitutes: DIY strips EUR 3-10, new blind EUR 29-80 (prior file's competitor list, unverified by me). Price EUR 35 sits above the substitute that fixes it for most people. VELUX's own DSL/side-channel blinds are the incumbent answer and VELUX controls installer channels.
5. SERIOUS: Window-code fit database means many SKUs (several VELUX sizes x blind generations x third-party blinds) on a EUR 8-10k first order: inventory spread thin, slow turns, dead stock. Return/mis-fit risk high for a "fits your exact code" product.
6. MODERATE: Seasonality. Pain peaks May-Aug in Nordics (midnight sun); orders in Nov launch miss the season by ~6 months, so first real demand signal is ~May 2027. Cash locked in stock meanwhile.
7. MODERATE: SOM 400-1,000 kits is itself an assumption with no installed-base data; TAM/SAM chain is all ASSUMPTION, Low confidence.
8. MODERATE: Compliance/consumer-law: GPSR operator, 14-day distance-selling returns (EU), adhesive/textile REACH, referential use of VELUX mark risk; EPR packaging registration in SE/DK for D2C packaging is a fixed cost not in the unit economics (not verified).
9. MINOR: Amazon EU as channel invites instant clones and private label.
