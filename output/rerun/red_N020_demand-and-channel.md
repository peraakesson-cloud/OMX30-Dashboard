# Red team N020 KeyPouch - demand and channel (2026-11-11)
Research limits: WebSearch budget exhausted; packagingeurope.com and eur-lex.europa.eu unresolvable from this session. NOTHING here is freshly verified; claims are from memory, labelled ASSUMPTION/Low-Medium.

## Attacks
1. FATAL-ish: the demand driver (PPWR minis restriction) is 2030 (memory: Reg 2025/40, Annex V, from 1 Jan 2030, Medium-Low), 3+ years out, and applies to SINGLE-USE minis only. Refillable bulk dispensers (Tork, Meadow, Bobrick, any pump bottle) already satisfy it. Law creates category demand for everyone, not for a keyed system. No urgency for 2027 buying.
2. SOM 25-45k EUR from ~600 cold prospects at 5-8% conversion: 10 h/week combined = ~500 h/yr; ~40% to sales for 600 prospects x multiple touches is not feasible; cold B2B reply rates 1-5% (unsourced) imply 6-30 replies, ~2-6 pilots, not 25-40 accounts. Realistic year-1 accounts: 3-10, revenue ~EUR 3-12k.
3. Buyer is wrong: small hotels/B&Bs buy amenities through wholesalers (HoReCa distributors) and decide on price/L and housekeeping time. Independent owner pays EUR 8-12/L vs bulk EUR 3-5/L (ASSUMPTION) = 2-3x; lock-in to a 2-person startup supplier (supply-continuity risk) is a stated objection, and the chosen vendor is a startup with no service network.
4. Ex-VELUX/Nilfisk networks: VELUX = building products/installers/housing associations, NOT hotel amenities buyers; Nilfisk = floor-care machines; janitorial distributors exist but already carry Tork/Katrin/Abena/Ecolab dispensing with free-dispenser-for-consumable-contract models. Founder "B2B channel they already have" claim is weak for this product.
5. Consumption/TAM: ~130 L/yr per 20-room property is an ASSUMPTION; 20 rooms x ~60% occupancy x 365 x ~1.5 guests x ~15-20 ml/guest-night = ~200-260 L shampoo+gel+soap total but split across 3 products, i.e. dispenser count 3 per bathroom or shared; per-account revenue EUR ~1,000/yr is plausible only if all three SKUs; pouch SKUs triple (shampoo, conditioner, body, hand) so MOQ/inventory burden multiplies. Fragrance/skin-feel complaints dominate guest reviews.
6. Repeat: seasonal (Nordic hotels low-season; STR churn), property closures, accounts switch on first price/leak issue; leaking pouches/clogged pumps in shipped 1 L formats are a service burden.
7. Regulatory pitfalls: filling cosmetics = Responsible Person, CPNP, safety assessor, GMP filler (EUR 0.5-1.5k+ per formula, more per SKU); EPR registration SE/DK for packaging; B2B so consumer-law exposure smaller, but dispensers need GPSR docs.
8. Moat: keyed interface likely excluded from design protection as technical must-fit (EU design reg 1/2002 art 8(2)); Sweden no utility model; Meadow KAPSUL and Tork already own cartridge lock-in patents/IP space (unverified, FTO risk). Capital 11-14k exceeds soft cap.
9. Competitor claims unverified: Meadow price not found; Paya listing is US generic.
