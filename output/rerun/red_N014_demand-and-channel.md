Research status: WebSearch budget exhausted, WebFetch blocked (bauhaus.se unresolvable). No new URLs verified. All attacks below are reasoning from the prior record; unverifiable items are marked.

Attacks
1. FATAL: Moat is nil. Prior record itself says defensibility 2 and "relationship barrier, not exclusivity". The ISO 16890 report is purchasable by any competitor; frame design must fit OEM housings. Under the re-run rules this has no qualifying moat.
2. SERIOUS: Compatible filters almost certainly already exist (prior record admits unverified). Filter-media converters (Camfil, Swegon-type distributors, generic Nordic webshops) cut to OEM sizes. Unverified by me.
3. SERIOUS: SOM is not credible for 10 h/week. 8-15 active installers x 15-30 sets at EUR 24 = EUR 3-11k revenue; contribution at EUR 12.5/set is EUR 1.5-5.6k. The D2C EUR 12-25 CAC is an unsourced assumption and exceeds EUR 15 contribution. Year-1 EUR 5-12k contribution is the optimistic tail; lab EUR 2-5k plus stock eats it.
4. SERIOUS: Installer channel misread. Founders' network is VELUX roofers/window installers, not ventilation service firms; prior record concedes "adjacent". Service firms earn recurring margin and warranty cover on OEM sets and have little reason to add an unknown SKU for ~EUR 6-20 saving per set. Per-installer value ~EUR 250/yr cannot pay for 4-6 founder hours plus sample sets.
5. SERIOUS: Repeat claim overstated. 65% retention is ASSUMPTION; OEM warranty language and unit service plans pull owners back to originals; subscription cancellation under EU consumer law (Directive 2023/2673 withdrawal button, applicable from June 2026, not re-verified) adds friction/cost.
6. MODERATE: Ticket EUR 39 is above the EUR 35 bar only marginally, and the product is a low-involvement consumable; price comparison on exact model keyword is trivial for buyers; Amazon/Google Shopping shows OEM and generic side by side.
7. MODERATE: Seasonality. Demand clusters pre-spring/autumn; first revenue spring 2027 means cash locked in 3 SKUs x 100 sets for 4-6 months. Many SKUs (Flexit, Genvex, Nilan, Systemair, Villavent, Swegon, Danvent, Brink) with different sizes; 3 SKUs covers a small slice of the installed base, so unmet-fit returns/complaints are likely.
8. MODERATE: Regulatory friction not costed: packaging EPR in SE and DK (and EU PPWR 2025/40 applying from Aug 2026; not verified), GPSR, trademark "compatible with" use, possible warranty/tying disputes, claims about allergy filtration limited to non-medical wording. Together a few hundred EUR plus admin hours.
9. MINOR: TAM EUR 400-700m and SAM have no source; contestable EUR 3-5m is assumption. Pollen-prevalence sources conflict.
10. MINOR: BRF channel has the payer/decision problem (see N015); board treasurers buy on price, and Hornbach EUR 10 generics anchor low.

Likely copiers: existing Nordic ventilation-parts webshops and filter distributors; Camfil/Pall-type converters; the OEMs themselves (price match, subscription); Amazon sellers of generic cut media.
