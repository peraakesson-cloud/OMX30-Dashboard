# I059 Magnetic Pane secondary glazing kit (+ frost/film refill variants) - deep analysis (re-run, 2026-11-11)

**Verdict: PARK as a consumer product; run a EUR 300-800 B2B pilot test only.** The pain is real and the founders' installer/housing-association access is a genuine asset, but the moat is a copyable size library, D2C paid acquisition is roughly break-even at best, and the "6-8 standard Nordic sizes" premise is unproven. Only worth capital if 2-3 housing associations/installers agree to a measured pilot.

## 1. Product and real pain
Pre-cut clear PET/acrylic pane + magnetic tape, no drilling, removable (record: EUR 29-49, landed EUR 8-12 at MOQ 300). Pain: cold, draughty, condensing older windows; renters cannot replace windows. Evidence is indirect forum chatter that film sags/fails ([MSE thread](https://forums.moneysavingexpert.com/discussion/6079776/secondary-glazing-film-any-good), [thecooldown](https://www.thecooldown.com/green-home/rotted-windows-insulation-solutions-renter/)).

Skeptical flags:
- **Landed cost looks optimistic.** A made-to-size 900x1200 mm acrylic pane plus magnet strip at EUR 8-12 landed is doubtful (ASSUMPTION: EUR 14-25 once cut-to-size, edge-finished, packed). Thin PET is cheaper but optically poorer and flexes.
- **"6-8 common Nordic sizes" is unvalidated.** Older Swedish/Danish stock has many non-standard sizes; Sweden's traditional coupled windows and Denmark's forsatsruder (secondary panes) already exist, so the stock the founders know best may be partly served by incumbent joiners (ASSUMPTION, background knowledge).
- **Physics/safety risk:** an inner pane tighter than the outer one is right; a leaky inner pane traps moist air and worsens condensation. Bedroom windows are escape routes; panes must be removable/non-obstructive.

## 3. Personas
1. **Renter, 28-45, Copenhagen/Aarhus/Stockholm/Gothenburg:** cold or noisy older flat, cannot alter windows, needs a deposit-safe fix. Pays EUR 40-150 total.
2. **Owner of 1930s-70s house/villa/lejlighed:** wants comfort before an EUR 8-15k window replacement; mild DIY, price-sensitive on multi-window orders.
3. **Property technician at a housing association/landlord (the B2B buyer):** wants a cheap, fast, tenant-friendly fix for complaint hotspots (north-facing flats, stairwells), needs documented performance and a supplier who can invoice and measure.
4. **Installer/glazier (ex-VELUX network):** wants an upsell or a rental-compatible stopgap; will resell if margin is 30-40% and sizing is handled.

## 7. Channel and CAC (ASSUMPTION)
- **D2C paid (Meta/TikTok, SE/DK):** CPC ~EUR 0.5-1.2, landing CVR 1.5-2.5% => **CAC ~EUR 25-70**. SEO is seasonal (Oct-Feb peak) and competes with film retailers. Low confidence.
- **Shopify + Amazon EU:** Amazon referral ~15% plus ads; fit-to-size is a poor Amazon fit and returns spike.
- **B2B (housing associations, installers):** CAC = founder time; sales cycle 3-9 months (ASSUMPTION; public/municipal housing needs tenders/framework agreements, small pilots may go under purchase thresholds). One signed 300-set pilot would outweigh everything else.

## 8. Regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- **GPSR (Regulation (EU) 2023/988):** economic operator/EU contact, labelling, risk assessment. Use flexible ferrite magnetic tape, not loose neodymium (swallowing hazard).
- **REACH / material compliance:** PET/acrylic, adhesives, magnet coating; supplier declarations.
- **Fire behaviour:** acrylic is combustible; B2B in multi-dwelling buildings may require a fire-class statement (national building rules, e.g. Swedish BBR / Danish BR18; ASSUMPTION - verify). Do not block escape windows.
- **Unfair Commercial Practices Directive / consumer-law savings claims:** no U-value, energy-bill or condensation claims without independent test (EN 673/ISO 10291-type measurement via an accredited lab; ASSUMPTION on exact standard).
- **Consumer Rights Directive:** 14-day withdrawal applies, but made-to-measure goods are exempt (Art. 16(c)); this favours custom-cut-to-order over stocked sizes for return risk.
- **Packaging EPR (SE, DK) and VAT/OSS** for cross-border B2C.
- CE marking under the Construction Products Regulation probably does not apply to a DIY add-on with no harmonised standard (ASSUMPTION; confirm).

## 9. The moat (honest)
- **Mechanism claimed:** B2B channel via ex-VELUX installers/housing associations + measured size library + test data.
- **How to obtain:** 20-40 window measurements in real buildings; accredited lab test (EUR 1.5-4k, ASSUMPTION, Medium-Low) for thermal/transmission; 2-3 reference pilots with housing associations; EU registered design (~EUR 350 filing, background knowledge, unverified) for the magnet/edge profile.
- **Cost/time:** EUR 2-5k and 4-9 months; the relationships are the only barrier and need founder time that competes with the 10 h/week budget.
- **Circumvention:** a competitor copies the kit in weeks (first4magnets already does a custom version); a registered design on a flat pane plus magnet tape is easy to design around; test data is a one-off cost any entrant can also pay. Only an incumbent installer, or a window OEM selling its own add-on, could remove the B2B channel advantage.
- **Defensibility score therefore 2:** the channel advantage is real but personal and non-exclusive; nothing legal prevents a copy.

## 11. Cheapest validation test (EUR 300-800, 4-6 weeks)
1. Make 3 sample sets from a local acrylic cutter (~EUR 150) and place in 3-5 real flats via the founders' contacts; log temperature/humidity with cheap loggers (~EUR 100).
2. Ask 12-15 housing-association technicians/installers for a 20-flat paid pilot at EUR 28/set; **success = 3+ yes/LOIs**.
3. In parallel a EUR 300 Meta smoke test (landing page, EUR 45 pre-order, size calculator): **success = pre-order/checkout rate >=1.5% of visitors** and AOV of 3+ sets.
4. Kill if fewer than 2 B2B conversations progress or measured benefit is not perceptible. No inventory before 2 signed pilots.
