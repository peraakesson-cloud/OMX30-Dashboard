## 6. Regulatory checklist (applicable only)
- Fertilising Products Reg. (EU) 2019/1009: microbial bran may count as a composting additive/microbial product (needs REACH registration dossier for additives; [EUR-Lex](https://eur-lex.europa.eu/eli/reg/2019/1009/2025-12-30/eng)); I could not confirm bokashi bran's classification. Get written confirmation before sale. Avoid fertiliser/soil-improver/odour/pathogen claims (else biocide or fertiliser rules).
- GPSR (2023/988) labelling, responsible EU person, traceability.
- CLP/REACH if blend contains classified substances (molasses/EM are usually fine; ASSUMPTION).
- Animal by-product / phytosanitary checks if bran imported from outside EU (ASSUMPTION; verify).
- Packaging EPR registration in SE (FTI) and DK, and packaging labelling; EU PPWR coming.
- Textile liner: textile labelling only if sold as a textile; otherwise GPSR.
- Not applicable: CE, food contact, cosmetics.
