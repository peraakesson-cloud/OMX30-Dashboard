## 7. Differentiation and copycat test
Possible: (a) tidy sill-matched profile (channel/drip lip with replaceable cartridge) sized for Nordic triple-pane and sills; (b) a "where does the water go" system plus humidity guidance; (c) landlord/housing-association bulk pack via building-industry contacts; (d) Scandi design and local-language guidance.
Copycat in 6 months: **easy.** Felt/non-woven and a soft extrusion die (~EUR 500-1500) are accessible to any Alibaba seller; refill format is not protectable except by a design registration of the profile (cheap but weak), and refill lock-in is easily beaten by generic tape sold per metre. Only moat candidates are the B2B relationship and brand/content, both small.
