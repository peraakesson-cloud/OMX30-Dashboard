- Product liability insurance (ASSUMPTION: needed in practice by Amazon).

## 7. Differentiation and copy-resistance
Possible angle: Nordic panel-radiator-fit design, matte-finish Scandi look, tested temperature rating. Weak. A bent steel shelf is copyable by any Chinese supplier within weeks (alibaba-style listing + Amazon undercut at EUR 12-18). Protections are only: EU registered design (cheap, easy to design around), brand/content/reviews, and Nordic-profile know-how from the VELUX/building background (small, not a moat). Honest defensibility: **2**.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTION unless noted)
- Retail EUR 29 incl. 25% VAT = EUR 23.2 net. Landed EUR 7.5 (phase-2 range 6-9). Gross margin ~68% net; retail/landed 3.9x. Passes the 65%/3x screens on paper.
- Less shipping/fulfilment to SE/DK ~EUR 4-6, payment ~0.8, returns ~0.5: contribution ~EUR 9-10 per unit before marketing. Blended CAC above ~EUR 9 makes it loss-making; paid CAC EUR 20-40 is deeply negative.
- First order: 500-600 units x EUR 7.5-8 = ~EUR 4-4.8k + tooling EUR 0.8k + safety test EUR 0.5-1k + freight ~EUR 0.3k = ~EUR 5.5-6.5k (at the cap).
- Year-1 SOM EUR 17-30k revenue implies profit near zero or negative after any ads.
