## 6. EU regulatory checklist
- GPSR (EU 2023/988): responsible person in EU, labelling, traceability - applies.
- REACH on filter media/adhesives/brush; RoHS not applicable (no electronics).
- Packaging EPR (SE/DK schemes) - applies at small scale; PPWR phasing.
- No CE marking expected (not a construction product per se), but caution: if sold as part of a window ventilation system or with airflow claims, harmonised construction-product/ventilation rules and national building codes may apply - check. Avoid mould-removal/biocide and health claims (Biocidal Products Regulation, medical-claim kill). Filter-fire-class not required for consumer accessory (ASSUMPTION).

## 7. Differentiation and copycat
Claimed: fit library for major window brands + annual refill. Honest view: a copycat needs only a laser/die cutter and a measurement table; Chinese sellers can clone in weeks. OEMs (Volution, Schellenberg, VILPE) can sell their own refills. The real moat would be the data on profile/vent compatibility plus installer/DIY-chain relationships - slow to build. Defensibility 2.
