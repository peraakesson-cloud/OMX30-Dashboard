## 2. Personas and jobs-to-be-done
1. **Design-minded gift buyer (28-45, urban SE/DK)**: wants a gift that signals taste, is not a plastic toy, and arrives in good packaging. Job: "give something thoughtful for EUR 30-45 without browsing 10 shops". Gift seasonality (Q4) dominates.
2. **Architecture/design professional or student (24-40)**: desk object, studio gift, nostalgia for model making. Job: "have a small building I can build and display". Likely to compare against LEGO Architecture and museum-shop paper models.
3. **Screen-free adult hobbyist (30-55)**: calming build, TikTok timelapse viewer (the Rolife/Robotime audience). Job: "a 3-6 hour mindful build". Skews to miniature rooms with furniture and lights, which a plain house shell does not satisfy.

## 6. EU regulatory checklist (applicable only)
- **GPSR (Reg. 2023/988)**: EU responsible person/contact and traceability markings are required for non-food consumer products sold online. Not verified in this research; check text.
- **Toy scope**: Position as 14+/adult to stay out of the Toy Safety Directive/EN71. Kid versions (I164/I191) require EN71 testing (~EUR 600-1,000, ASSUMPTION from I191 record) and the new Toy Safety Regulation 2025/2509 (PFAS/bisphenol bans, digital product passport; applies Aug 2030) ([SGS](https://www.sgs.com/news/2025/12/safeguards-18725-eu-toy-safety-regulation-2025-2509-published)).
- **REACH/chemicals**: inks, glue, coatings (cardboard/plywood adhesives, formaldehyde in birch ply); supplier declarations. If LED "light box" is added: LVD/EMC/RoHS and WEEE, low-voltage USB only, avoid batteries (hard kill).
- **Packaging**: PPWR (Reg. 2025/40) applies largely from 12 Aug 2026 incl. EPR registration/producer responsibility organisation per country ([White & Case](https://www.whitecase.com/insight-alert/application-packaging-and-packaging-waste-regulation-ppwr-further-practical-guidance)); verify authorised-representative requirements for cross-border sellers. Sweden and Denmark each have national schemes (not verified).
- **FSC claim** only if chain-of-custody certified; otherwise do not claim.
- **IP**: real buildings: architect copyright (life of author + 70 years), and trademark/design rights on landmarks vary by country. Use pre-1950s/public domain or licensed buildings, or original fictional designs.
- Not applicable: food contact, cosmetics, biocides.

## 7. Differentiation and copycat test
Possible edge: original Nordic house designs with real daylight/roof-window logic (ex-VELUX), sun-path content, hand-off to building-industry channels (showrooms, architecture schools).
Copycat test, 6 months: **weak.** Laser/die-cut files can be reproduced from a photo; Chinese factories and Etsy sellers can copy a design in weeks; free laser-cut house files already circulate. Design copyright protects the artwork, not the idea, and enforcement by two part-timers across Amazon/Etsy is impractical. The only durable assets are a licensed partnership (e.g. a daylight/architecture brand or museum) and brand/community, neither of which exists on day 1. Honest defensibility: 2.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTIONS unless noted)
- Retail EUR 39 incl. 25% VAT (SE/DK) = EUR 31.2 net.
- Landed cost EUR 6-9 at 500-1,000 units (I049 record "5-9 estimate"); take EUR 7.5.
- Gross margin on net price: (31.2 - 7.5) / 31.2 = **~76%**; landed multiple 4.2x. Passes the 65%/3x targets on paper.
- After pick/pack/ship to SE/DK (EUR 6-8), payment fees (~3%), packaging EPR: contribution ~EUR 15-17 per unit (~50%). Paid CAC of EUR 25-45 exceeds this: paid acquisition loses money on first purchase, and repeat purchase is low (one-off kit), so profit relies on organic reach or wholesale.
- First order: 500 units x EUR 7.5 = EUR 3.75k, plus tooling EUR 200-600, prototypes/photography EUR 500-1,000, compliance EUR 300-1,000 = **~EUR 4.8-6.4k all-in**, within the EUR 6k cap but leaving little for ads.
- Break-even: needs ~300+ units sold organically to recover the first order; Year-1 SOM of 400-1,000 units gives EUR 6-16k of contribution before ads and founder time.

## Verdict
**Park / conditional.** Do not place a EUR 5k production order. If pursued at all, run a EUR 500-1,000 validation: 2 designs, laser-cut prototypes, pre-sale page and TikTok timelapses, with a gate of ~100 pre-orders at EUR 35+ before any production. The Daylight Lab (kids) variant is better differentiated by founders' expertise but adds EN71/toy regulation and a seasonal gift market.

**Biggest risk:** no defensible edge plus occupied shelf space (Zupagrafika, Litogami, Rolife, LEGO Architecture at the same EUR 40 price), with a one-purchase product that cannot absorb paid CAC.
