## 6. EU regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- GPSR (EU 2023/988, applying since 13 Dec 2024): EU-based manufacturer/responsible-person details, address and email on product/pack, batch/ref, warnings, risk assessment and technical file (https://www.obelis.net/other-industries/gpsr/ ; https://ecommercegermany.com/blog/gpsr-general-product-safety-regulation/). If the factory is in China and the founders are the brand owner in the EU, they are the manufacturer for GPSR.
- Burn/fire/hot-wax warnings and lighter/candle-warmer safety; an open-flame warmer or tea light raises risk. Prefer supplying no flame source or a CE-marked electric melter (extra LVD/EMC/RoHS work - avoid).
- REACH/CLP: lead and other metals in brass (Annex XVII restrictions, lead SVHC), wax colourants and fragrance (CLP labelling if scented). Get supplier test reports (ASSUMPTION: EUR 300-800 for lab tests).
- Packaging EPR: Sweden (FTI/producer responsibility), Denmark (packaging EPR registration) and Germany VerpackG/LUCID if selling on Amazon.de. FSC claims need chain-of-custody from the paper supplier.
- Not applicable: CE (no applicable harmonised directive for plain stamp set, unless electric melter), food contact, cosmetics, biocides, batteries. Toys: market as adult product; a child-appeal motif could trigger the Toy Safety Directive (ASSUMPTION).

## 7. Differentiation and copyability
Idea: curated Scandi motif heads (swappable), a signature wax palette, FSC envelopes, gift box. A copycat can replicate in <6 months: brass dies from Chinese suppliers cost EUR 20-60 each, and designs are easily imitated visually; copyright/registered design (EUR ~70-100 EU registered design per design, ASSUMPTION) only stops direct copies. Refill wax is a commodity (generic sticks/beads EUR 3-11 seen above), and a swappable-head standard has no lock-in unless heads are proprietary and cheap to add. Realistic moats: brand/community and collaborations with Nordic illustrators; neither is durable at this budget.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTIONS)
- Retail EUR 34 incl. 25% Swedish/Danish VAT = EUR 27.2 net. Landed cost EUR 7-10 (brass head, handle, 3 wax sticks, 5 envelopes, gift box, freight; supplier's EUR 6-9 not verified) plus die tooling EUR 150-400 per motif.
- Gross margin ~63-74% on net revenue; ~3.4-3.9x landed. Meets the margin target only if EUR 34 holds against EUR 4-27 competitors.
- Contribution after pick/pack/shipping ~EUR 4-6, payments ~3%, and CAC EUR 20-35: **about -EUR 5 to -EUR 18 per first order**. Positive only via organic reach or refill repeat, and refills (EUR 9-12 retail, ~EUR 1-2 cost) are bought rarely because one stick makes ~10 seals.
- First order: 500 sets x EUR 8 = EUR 4.0k + 5 motif dies EUR 1.0-1.5k + tests/packaging design ~EUR 0.5k = ~EUR 5.5-6k (at the budget limit; phase-2's EUR 3-3.5k assumed fewer units). Die cost stays under EUR 2k, so not a near-kill.

## Verdict
Do not make this the core bet. Gross margin looks fine but the category is a crowded, low-price craft commodity, copying is trivial, repeat is weak, CAC likely exceeds first-order contribution, and the founder edges (building industry, robotics cleaning) do not apply. If pursued, run a EUR 500-1,000 test: 2 motifs, pre-sell via Shopify/TikTok, measure organic traction before ordering.

## Biggest risk
Price compression and imitation: a EUR 34 set must prove value against EUR 4-27 kits, with CAC above contribution on first order.
