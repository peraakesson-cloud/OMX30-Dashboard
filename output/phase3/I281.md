## 7. Differentiation and copycat risk
Possible: premium Nordic design handle (metal/bamboo), a clean-tear compostable refill, subscription. Copycat in 6 months: trivial. Stock handle + generic refills mean no tooling or patent moat; "own adhesive formulation" is unrealistic for a 5-10k project; a design patent on handle-core interface protects only the shape and would be sidestepped. IKEA and Muji already own the refill-format price point. Defensibility 1/5.

## Biggest risk
