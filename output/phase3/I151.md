## 2. Personas and jobs-to-be-done
1. Urban design-minded single cat owner, 25-40 (Stockholm/Copenhagen): wants cat gear that does not look like plastic clutter; job: "keep my indoor cat stimulated without ugly toys".
2. Gift buyer / new-kitten owner: job: "a cute, safe, giftable set"; one-off, low refill intent.
3. Eco-minded owner annoyed by landfill toys: job: "stop buying disposable toys"; the refill story fits but willingness to pay a subscription is unproven.
Skeptical note: the real job (fresh scent) is solved by a EUR 3-4 bag of loose catnip/silvervine; refill subscription is a convenience tax.

## 7. Differentiation and copycat test
Ownable: Nordic design language, mono-material washable sew, branded refill format (sealed silvervine pouch + stitched-in pocket), EU-based supply with Swedish/Danish UX. What stops a copycat in 6 months: nothing structural. No patent path (a pocket and a pouch), no tooling moat, Chinese sewing factories will clone designs; KONG/Gigwi can add SKUs. Only soft moats: brand/community and subscription habit. Defensibility 2 is generous.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTION unless noted)
- Toy EUR 19 retail incl. 25% VAT (SE/DK) = EUR 15.2 net; landed 2.5-4 (record) -> ~74-84% product GM, but after shipping (~EUR 4-5 in Nordics), payment fees (~3%) and pick/pack, contribution ~EUR 7-8.
- Refill EUR 10 incl. VAT = 8 net; landed ~1.15; letterbox post ~EUR 2.5-3; contribution ~EUR 4.
- Retail >= EUR 15 satisfied only via the toy; refill alone is below the price floor (sell as bundle, as the audit says).
- First order: 1,000 toys x ~3.25 = 3.3k + 1,000 refills x 1.15 = 1.2k + samples/tests/packaging/GPSR 1-1.5k = ~EUR 5.5-6k at a 1,000-unit MOQ; a 300-unit pilot is ~EUR 2-2.5k (consistent with the screen's 1.5-2.5k). Under the EUR 6k cap but leaves almost no marketing budget.
