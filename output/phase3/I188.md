# I188 - Repair-first mending kit (bags, shoes, jackets)

Date: 2026-11-11. Overall confidence: **Low-Medium**. Limits: Firecrawl credits ran out, fashionunited.uk was blocked by the network, and only the generic WebSearch tool worked. I did NOT open Amazon.de/.se, Etsy or Nordic retailer pages or any review pages. Review themes and Nordic prices are therefore ASSUMPTION or not verified.

## Verdict
**Weak / do not pursue as a standalone SKU.** It passes the capital and regulatory gates but fails on defensibility and paid-acquisition economics. Keep only as a bundle or accessory, or as a B2B co-brand idea to test cheaply.
Biggest risk: a EUR 29-39 kit of commodity components (awl, thread, patches) is copied in weeks, and a EUR 10-15 near-equivalent already sits on Amazon.

## 1. Market size (all bottom-up figures are ASSUMPTION)
- Related spend, for context only: Europe's clothing and footwear repair market (services) is about EUR 2.8bn in 2025, growing about 5.5% a year to about EUR 3.7bn by 2030 (FMC x KPMG, via secondary coverage: https://www.globaltextiletimes.com/?p=279735 , study page https://transition-pathways.europa.eu/textiles/library/circular-fashion-market-study-europe-circular-fashion-federation-x-kpmg-2025). I did not open the primary report. This is professional services, not DIY kits, so it is not the TAM.
- A Springer study (https://link.springer.com/article/10.1007/s43615-025-00596-2), as summarised by search, says consumers express willingness to repair but actual participation is low, and that cost, not environment, is the main motivator. That is a warning for a premium-priced kit.
- **TAM EU (EUR ~150M, ASSUMPTION, Low confidence):** about 200M households x 3% buying a mending or leather-care style kit per year x EUR 25.
- **SAM Nordics (EUR ~3-9M, ASSUMPTION):** about 12M Nordic households x 3% x EUR 25 gives about 9M. Only about a third of that is plausibly "design-led premium", so about 3M.
- **SOM year 1 (EUR ~15-30k revenue, ASSUMPTION):** 500-1,000 kits at about EUR 30 net. Founder-hours (10 per week combined) limit this, not demand.
- Demand evidence is thin. The idea file itself says no verified repair-kit demand trend was found (policy inference only). Trend evidence links are ESPR policy items, not purchase data.

## 2. Personas and jobs-to-be-done (ASSUMPTION, no primary research)
1. **"Buy-it-for-life" urban 28-50 (Stockholm/Copenhagen):** owns EUR 150+ boots, a leather bag and a shell jacket. Job: extend the life of a loved item without a trip to a cobbler. Realistic frequency is one repair a year.
2. **Gift buyer for a "sustainable" friend:** job is a thoughtful, design-led gift at EUR 30-40. This is probably the strongest real use case, and it is seasonal (Q4).
3. **Outdoor/commuter tinkerer:** job is a field fix for a jacket or pack. Already served by Tenacious Tape and Fjällräven's kit.
Skeptic's note: most urban owners will pay a cobbler or buy new. A kit mostly satisfies intent rather than a repeating need.

## 4. Review gaps (NOT VERIFIED, hypotheses to test)
I read no 1-3 star reviews. Plausible themes of cheap awl kits (ASSUMPTION): awl or needle quality, unclear instructions, wrong thread colour, no patches matched to the material. Real room for a better guide plus QR video, but this is a content edge, not a hardware moat.

## 5. Channels and CAC
- Benchmarks (vendor blogs, directional): Sweden Meta CPM about EUR 9.25, and DACH/Nordics apparel DTC CAC EUR 60-110 per eightx (https://eightx.co/blog/average-eu-ecommerce-cac-by-country-2026). Triple Whale median Meta CPA about USD 38 (https://www.triplewhale.com/blog/facebook-ads-benchmarks), US-weighted.
- At EUR 18-20 gross profit per order, paid acquisition cannot be profitable. Only organic TikTok/Instagram (mending content, "repair" creators), gifting and marketplace search are viable. Blended CAC assumption EUR 15-25 organic-led, with high variance.
- Amazon EU: the category is crowded with EUR 6-15 sets. Amazon fees are about 15% plus fulfilment (ASSUMPTION), so a EUR 29 price likely needs strong ratings first.

## 6. Regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- **GPSR (EU 2023/988):** applies. A sharp awl means a risk assessment, warnings, "not for children", an EU responsible person, and batch/ID marking.
- **REACH:** restricted substances in dyes (azo), leather (Cr VI), metals (nickel release in needles), and phthalates in any plastics.
- **CLP:** applies only if glue is included. Recommended: omit adhesive.
- **EPR packaging:** SE and DK registrations, plus the PPWR phase-in.
- **Not applicable:** CE (no radio/electrical), food contact, cosmetics, biocides, batteries. WEEE not applicable.
Source for policy context: ESPR/destruction-ban links in the idea record (https://transition-pathways.europa.eu/textiles/news/new-rules-stop-destruction-unsold-clothes-and-shoes-enter-application-fostering-eu). No dated rule creates demand for consumer repair kits.

## 7. Differentiation and copycat test
Ownable (weak): design, instructions and QR video, a refill subscription, curated material-specific patches, and brand co-branding. A copycat can replicate the box, content and bundle in about 6 weeks on Alibaba. What could last: a B2B channel (brands needing a repair offer under ESPR sell your co-branded kit), and a content library with a community. Neither is proven.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTION unless noted)
- Retail EUR 29-39 incl. VAT (idea file). At EUR 34 and 25% VAT the net is about EUR 27.2.
- Landed cost EUR 6-10 in the idea file. With a printed box, QR guide, patches and EU responsible-person costs, I assume EUR 9-11.
- Gross margin on own Shopify: (27.2 - 10) / 27.2 = about 63%, or about 2.7x landed. This misses the 65% / 3x targets once real costs are in. At EUR 39 retail it is about 69% / 3.2x.
- Amazon: after about 15% fee plus fulfilment, the contribution is thin.
- First order: 300-500 units x EUR 9-11 = EUR 2.7-5.5k, plus compliance testing of about EUR 300-800 (ASSUMPTION). Within the EUR 6k cap; no tooling.

## Recommendation
Do not commit capital as a hero product. If kept, run a EUR 500 test: a 50-unit gift-season batch, content-led, plus 3 outreach calls to Nordic outdoor/bag brands about a co-branded repair offer (the founders' negotiation strength).
