# I132 - Nordic Techo Planner + Refills: deep market and customer analysis (2026-11-11)

Evidence note: Firecrawl was out of credits and the Amazon/Etsy/social platforms could not be scraped, so only WebSearch snippets were available. Anything not tied to a URL below is labelled ASSUMPTION. Overall confidence: LOW to MEDIUM.

## Verdict
CONDITIONAL / PARK UNLESS A CHEAP DEMAND TEST PASSES. Cheap to start (about EUR 3-5k first run, no tooling) and refills give repeat, but defensibility is weak (2/5): a Nordic-language layout is easy to copy, the hero product (Hobonichi) is already sold in Sweden, and the key input (Tomoe River-type paper) is a single-source Japanese supply with gated wholesale pricing. Biggest risk: paper supply and a commodity-copy window. Timing: it is 11 Nov 2026, so the 2027 season is effectively gone; the realistic launch is a 2028 edition or an Aug 2027 school-year edition (ASSUMPTION).

## 6. EU regulatory checklist (only applicable items)
- GPSR (Reg. 2023/988): paper stationery is a consumer product; EU-based operator, traceability (batch/type), contact details and safety info on pack or product. No CE.
- PPWR (Reg. 2025/40): applies from 12 Aug 2026 (https://www.globalelr.com/2026/08/eu-packaging-and-packaging-waste-regulation-begins-application/); shipping boxes and any wrap are packaging; EPR registration needed per country for cross-border distance sales, check Naturvardsverket (SE) and Miljostyrelsen (DK) (https://www.spscommerce.com/community/articles/one-region-two-ppwr-deadlines-what-nordic-retailers-need-to-know-as-of-august-2026).
- REACH/inks: printing inks and any cover coating or leather alternative should come with supplier declarations; avoid phthalates/azo claims we cannot back (supplier declaration, ASSUMPTION).
- EUDR (wood-based paper) potentially applies to paper from operators; check with the printer (ASSUMPTION, verify).
- FSC/PEFC claim: use only with chain-of-custody certification of printer.
- Consumer law: 14-day withdrawal right online, 2-year guarantee, VAT OSS for cross-border (SE/DK 25%).
- Not applicable: batteries, food contact, cosmetics, biocides, medical.

## 7. Differentiation and copycat test
Own: Swedish/Danish layouts (vecka numbers, school terms, red days, name days), local-currency pricing with no import duty, a refill-first system (annual refill about EUR 19), designer cover collaborations, community/ritual content.
Copycat in 6 months: easy. Any print-on-demand shop, Burde/Mayland (they already do Swedish day-books), or Etsy sellers can add a Nordic layout. The cover/refill lock-in only works if the cover size is proprietary and the community is large. Hobonichi can also add Nordic holiday data or a Nordic distributor. Defensibility is therefore weak: 2/5.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTION unless stated)
- Retail EUR 35 cover+refill bundle (EUR 29-39 range from the screen), refill EUR 19. SE/DK VAT 25%, so EUR 35 gross = EUR 28 net.
- Landed cost EUR 6-10 (screen estimate, MOQ 500; unverified); use EUR 9 for planning. Net margin EUR 19 = 68% bundle; refill-only EUR 19 gross = EUR 15.2 net on about EUR 4-5 refill cost = about 70%. Retail multiple on landed cost: about 3.9x gross (clears 3x), about 3.1x net of VAT (borderline). Margin test against wholesale to retail (50% off) fails: EUR 14 net vs EUR 9 cost = 36%. Retail later is off the target.
- Costs not in landed: pick/pack/shipping to SE/DK about EUR 5-7, payments about 3%, returns, so contribution about EUR 11-13 per bundle before CAC. With paid CAC EUR 15-25, first purchase loses money.
- First order: 500 units x EUR 9 = about EUR 4.5k plus samples, packaging, EPR and design = EUR 5-6k, inside the EUR 6k ceiling but with no buffer. Single-source paper wholesale price unknown (Sanzen/Yamamoto, https://www.superdelivery.com/en/r/pd_p/11205278/ shows members-only wholesale pricing).
