## 6. EU regulatory checklist (applicable only; not legal advice)
- **GPSR (Reg. 2023/988, applies since 13 Dec 2024):** EU-based manufacturer/responsible person, product identification (batch/type), contact details on product or pack, safety information in local languages (Swedish, Danish): https://www.complir.io/resources/guides/gpsr-compliance-guide-product-companies ; https://webinterpret.com/en?p=377354 . If the factory is non-EU, the founders' EU company is the importer/responsible person.
- **Sharp-needle hazard:** the punch needle is a pointed tool; document risk assessment, add warnings and age grading (adult 14+/16+). Marketing to children would trigger the Toy Safety Directive/EN71: avoid.
- **REACH:** restricted substances (azo dyes releasing aromatic amines, SVHC) in yarn/fabric, plus metal keyring hardware (nickel release, EN 1811 for items in prolonged skin contact; lead/cadmium in jewellery-type metal). Get supplier test reports.
- **Textile Labelling Regulation (EU 1007/2011):** fibre composition on yarn packs sold as textile products. Likely applies to yarn refills.
- **Packaging EPR:** Sweden (FTI/producer responsibility registration) and Denmark packaging schemes; PPWR (Reg. 2025/40) phased in from 2026. Register or use a marketplace/3PL service.
- **WEEE/Battery:** not applicable (no electronics). **Biocides/cosmetics/food contact:** not applicable.
- **Distance-selling consumer law:** 14-day withdrawal, VAT OSS (Sweden/Denmark 25%), accessible product info.
- **IP:** originality of motifs; avoid Marimekko/Royal Copenhagen-style lookalikes; register EU designs only if cheap.

## 7. Differentiation and copycat reality
Own: (a) an editorial Nordic/architectural motif library (founder design + building-industry edge: roof/window/facade details, Copenhagen/Stockholm landmarks), (b) a "wearable result" finished with proper hardware, (c) a drop cadence and refill yarn palette. What stops a copycat in 6 months: honestly very little. Needles, fabric and yarn are commodities; copyright covers specific artwork only, not the style or concept; any Etsy shop or a Panduro/Hobbii private label could ship similar kits in weeks; kit tool "setups" are unpatentable. The moat is brand/community and design taste, which takes more than 6 months and more time than 10 h/week allows. Refill packs lock in only if the yarn palette is proprietary and the charm is a recurring collectible (unproven).
