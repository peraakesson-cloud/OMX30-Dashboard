# I061 - Nordic wood care bar (cluster: I061 + I225 "Birch Board Care Kit")

Date: 2026-11-11. Overall confidence: **Low-Medium**. Firecrawl credits were exhausted and several Nordic retailer pages could not be fetched (knivesandtools.dk, glashusetmalmo.se not reachable in this session), so competitor evidence comes from web-search snippets only. Anything not linked is labelled ASSUMPTION.

## Verdict
**Weak / conditional. Do not pick as the lead product.** Margins on paper are excellent, but the product is a commodity (food-safe oil + wax) with a EUR 5-12 anchor price in DIY stores, a clear exact-match competitor already on the market, no verifiable trend growth, and first-order CAC that exceeds first-order gross profit. Viable only as a low-effort add-on or bundle with boards/utensils, not as a standalone brand.

**Biggest risk:** price anchoring. The customer compares a EUR 19-24 tin with EUR 6-12 DIY-store oils and EUR 5-9 generic beeswax products; the formulation is not protectable and "Nordic design" is a thin moat.

## 6. EU regulatory checklist (only what applies)
- **GPSR (Reg. 2023/988):** EU responsible person/address, batch/lot, safety info and manufacturer details on product or packaging. Applies to consumer products sold online.
- **Food-contact materials: Reg. 1935/2004 and GMP Reg. 2023/2006:** wax/oil applied to boards that touch food; keep supplier declarations of compliance (food-grade oil, wax), traceability. Phase 2 note (not independently verified by me): no specific EU measure for waxes; strictly it is a coating of food-contact articles, so treat compliance file as precautionary.
- **CLP (Reg. 1272/2008):** rapeseed oil + beeswax/carnauba should be non-hazardous; any essential-oil scent could trigger classification/labelling. Keep unscented.
- **Cosmetics Reg. 1223/2009:** avoid skin/hand claims; do not market as balm.
- **Biocidal Products Reg.:** no "antibacterial/disinfecting" claims.
- **Packaging EPR:** tin and carton: Germany VerpackG with LUCID registration; marketplaces require proof (https://www.verpackungsregister.org/en/lucid-packaging-register ; one vendor note: https://retailboss.co/lucid-number-definition-and-how-to-get-it); Sweden and Denmark have their own producer responsibility schemes (ASSUMPTION: registration via FTI in SE and a DK scheme; not verified here); EU PPWR (2025/40) labelling/recycled-content obligations now phasing in (ASSUMPTION, check dates).
- **Cosmetics notification (CPNP):** not required if positioned as non-cosmetic (Phase 2 audit).
- No CE, no batteries, no REACH SVHC issues expected for tin (check tin coating supplier declaration; ASSUMPTION).

## 7. Differentiation they could own and copycat resistance
Candidate angles: (a) rapeseed-oil + beeswax/carnauba plant-forward formula (no mineral oil) with explicit "do not use flaxseed/olive oil" guidance; (b) Scandinavian tin + birch applicator + refill bar; (c) bundles with Nordic board makers.
Honest assessment: (a) is already sold by Holzland (beeswax, sugarcane wax, rapeseed oil) and Nourishing (plant-based, Swedish); (b) is copyable by any private-label supplier in under 6 months (tins/labels/applicators are catalog items); (c) is the only partially defensible one (exclusive board-maker partnerships). No patents, no tooling moat. Defensibility rests on brand, content and partnerships, which a two-founder part-time team cannot build quickly.

## 8. Rough unit economics (all ASSUMPTION unless linked)
- Retail EUR 22 incl. 25% VAT (SE/DK) = EUR 17.60 net. Phase 2 price band EUR 19-24.
- Landed cost EUR 3.5-5 per 100 g tin (oil, wax, tin, label, box, freight). Phase 2 said 3-5. Gross margin on net about 72-80%; approx. 3.6-5x landed on net price.
- After payment fees (about 3%), parcel to consumer (EUR 4-7 to SE/DK, letterbox) and pick/pack: contribution EUR 6-10 per single-tin order before ads. With CAC EUR 25-40: loses EUR 15-30 per first order.
- Bundle (tin + applicator + board brush) at EUR 39: net EUR 31, landed EUR 8-9: contribution about EUR 17 before ads. Still marginal at CAC 30.
- **First order:** 1,000 tins x EUR 3.5-5 = EUR 3.5-5k incl. tins/labels; Phase 2 estimate EUR 1.5-2.5k looks low unless MOQ about 500. No new mould needed (stock tins), so tooling stays under EUR 2k.
- Repeat: a 100 g tin likely lasts 6-12 months for a typical household (ASSUMPTION), so Phase 2's repeat 5 is too generous.
