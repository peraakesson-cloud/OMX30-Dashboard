# I003 - Nordic pantry label kit (SV/DA): deep analysis (2026-11-11)

Cluster: I003 + I027 (Pre-Printed Nordic Label System) + I056 (Nordic Pantry & Drawer Label Refill System).
Evidence limits: Firecrawl out of credits; WebFetch blocked for etsy.com, ikea.com, lieblingsglas.de (not on session allowlist). Only search-snippet evidence was available. Prices/reviews not scraped live. Anything not sourced is marked ASSUMPTION.

## Verdict
**Weak / do not pursue as a standalone hero product (Low-Medium confidence).** Margin and capital are fine; demand is unproven, defensibility is about 1-2/5, and the nearest substitutes are free or bundled (container sets ship with free labels, DIY printables, IKEA/Jula/ICA labels). Only viable as a cheap add-on or test ("fake door" at <EUR 500) rather than a EUR 6k bet.
Biggest risk: nobody has to pay EUR 17 for text stickers when labels are bundled with jars or printable for free; any Etsy seller can translate the set in a weekend.

## 6. Regulatory (EU) checklist
- GPSR (Reg. 2023/988): EU responsible person/address on product or pack, traceability, product info; applies to any consumer product sold in EU.
- Adhesive/vinyl chemistry: REACH SVHC compliance declaration from supplier; stickers are not food-contact if kept off food surfaces; do not claim "food safe" (avoid; if labels touch food-contact surfaces of jars, request migration declaration under Reg. 1935/2004 as precaution).
- Packaging EPR: Sweden (FTI/producer responsibility register), Denmark (DPA/packaging EPR from 2025) and PPWR (Reg. 2025/40) registration and fees for sales packaging; minimal for envelope pack. Confirm per country (not verified here).
- Not applicable: CE, cosmetics, biocides, batteries, medical claims.
- Language labelling: SV/DA instructions for use. VAT: OSS for cross-border B2C; SE 25%, DK 25% (ASSUMPTION).

## 7. Differentiation and copycat test
Ownable: complete SV/DA pantry vocabulary (incl. regional terms), type system, size-matched to common jars (IKEA/Weck/Kilner-type), refill packs, matching sets by cabinet/freezer/drawer. Copycat in 6 months: any Etsy seller or printer can translate and mimic in days; design is not patentable (EU registered design possible, cheap, but hard to enforce against marketplaces). Nothing durable. Only moats: brand/community, retail listing (e.g. Clas Ohlson/Jula) and bundling with jar makers; none exist at start.

## 8. Unit economics (ASSUMPTION unless noted)
- Retail EUR 17 incl. 25% VAT = EUR 13.60 net (floor of brief: >= EUR 15 ok). Landed EUR 2.5-4 (from ideas file; digital print/die-cut, no tooling).
- Gross margin ex VAT 71-82%; ratio retail/landed 4.3-6.8x (meets >=3x, >=65%).
- After variable costs: letter shipping to SE/DK ~EUR 3-4, payment 3% (~EUR 0.5), packaging ~EUR 0.4 -> contribution ~EUR 5-7 per order before ads; a paid CAC of EUR 15+ makes it loss-making.
- First order: 1.5-2.5k EUR (ideas file) for ~600-1,000 sets; no tooling (die EUR 100-300).
- 1,000 orders/year = ~EUR 5-7k contribution; fine for 10 h/week only as hobby-scale.
